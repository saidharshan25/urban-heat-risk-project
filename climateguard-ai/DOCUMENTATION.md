# ClimateGuard AI — System Documentation & Operations Manual

## 1. System Overview
ClimateGuard AI is a Chennai Heat Hazard Surveillance and Scenario Simulator designed for biometeorological hazard monitoring, spatial planning optimization, and precautionary scenario simulations.

---

## 2. Local Startup Commands & Required Ports

### A. Dashboard Server
- **Port**: `8080` (Default: `http://127.0.0.1:8080`)
- **Startup Command**:
  ```bash
  cd climateguard-ai
  python src/dashboard/server.py --port 8080
  ```
- **Served Interface**: Serves `src/dashboard/static/index.html` dynamically at `/` and provides read-only telemetry reports at `/api/report`.

### B. SAI Agent API
- **Port**: `8081` (Default: `http://127.0.0.1:8081`)
- **Startup Command**:
  ```bash
  cd Agent/aws-agent
  python -m uvicorn agent_api:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8081
  ```
- **Endpoints**:
  - `GET /api/agent/health`: Health status and external notification flag.
  - `POST /api/agent/scenario`: H3 spatial scenario simulation.
  - `POST /api/agent/advisory`: Precautionary biometeorological advisory generation.
  - `GET /api/agent/outputs`: Tree planting & emergency team optimization statuses.
  - `POST /api/agent/alert`: Local simulated incident logger.

---

## 3. Main Dashboard Features
1. **Surveillance Telemetry**: 168-hour biometeorological forecast with interactive SVG timeline (168h, 48h, 24h zoom).
2. **Risk Tier Classifications**: Categorized into Green (Normal), Yellow (Caution), Orange (Heat Alert), and Red (Severe Heat Alert).
3. **Scenario Simulator Engine**: Evaluates Baseline, Heatwave (+20%), and Extreme Heat (+35%) impacts across 3,859 Chennai H3 hexagons.
4. **Precautionary Advisory Generator**: Produces draft-only biometeorological screening advice with official public health resource links.
5. **Spatial Planning Outputs**: Interactive paginated tables for tree-planting priority (1,000 hexes) and emergency-team placement (2,500 facilities) with direct CSV exports.
6. **Simulated Alert Activity Log**: Real-time incident feed logging local events with `SIMULATED_NOT_SENT` status.
7. **Accessibility & Responsive Design**: WCAG 2.2 AA compliant, screen-reader polite live announcer (`#sr-announcer`), `:focus-visible` outlines, reduced motion support, and adaptive layouts across viewports from 320px to 1600px.

---

## 4. Existing Data Sources & Limitations
- **Numerical Weather Data**: Open-Meteo numerical weather forecasts (13.0827°N, 80.2707°E) with 1-hour temporal resolution.
- **H3 Spatial Vulnerability**: Hexagonal spatial indices (Resolution 8) incorporating vulnerable demographics, land surface temperature, and canopy deficits.
- **Limitations**:
  - Model outputs represent screening approximations, not real-time physical ground weather station measurements.
  - Advisory texts are demonstration screening guidelines and do not constitute clinical medical advice.

---

## 5. Illustrative Scenario Multipliers
- **Baseline (1.00x)**: Standard seasonal baseline risk index.
- **Heatwave (1.20x)**: Elevated sustained temperatures representing consecutive days above threshold heat indices.
- **Extreme Heat (1.35x)**: Severe acute peak temperatures evaluating peak load on emergency medical facilities and shaded water points.

---

## 6. Precautionary Advisory & Official Directives
Advisories generated by the dashboard are strictly **DRAFT ONLY — NOT OFFICIAL WARNINGS**. Users and responders must consult authorized governmental directives:
- **India Meteorological Department (IMD)**: [https://mausam.imd.gov.in](https://mausam.imd.gov.in)
- **Tamil Nadu State Disaster Management Authority (TNDSMA)**: [https://tnsdma.tn.gov.in](https://tnsdma.tn.gov.in)
- **National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)**: [https://www.ndma.gov.in](https://www.ndma.gov.in)
- **Tamil Nadu Directorate of Public Health & Preventive Medicine (TN DPH)**: [https://nhm.tn.gov.in](https://nhm.tn.gov.in)

---

## 7. Simulated Alert Behavior
- Alert logging is strictly local (`SIMULATED_NOT_SENT`).
- External notifications are permanently disabled (`external_notification_sent: false`).
- No external SMS, Webhook, WhatsApp, or civil defence broadcast is ever dispatched.

---

## 8. Known Issues & Future Improvements
- **Raw GeoJSON Ingestion**: Re-running ingestion integration tests requires manual provision of `chennai_tamil_nadu_india_boundary.geojson` in `data/raw/`.
- **Forecast Service Date Assertion**: Unit test `test_forecast_risk_service.py` expects hardcoded date `2026-10-10`, whereas fresh live cached reports dynamically reflect current timestamps.
